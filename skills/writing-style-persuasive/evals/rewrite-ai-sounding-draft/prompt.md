Rewrite this announcement as a persuasive, human-sounding message for local parents. Keep every fact and the calm, welcoming intent. Do not add facts, testimonials, or invented personal experiences. Return only the polished announcement.

Draft:

We are thrilled to announce an exciting new chapter for our neighborhood library! For months, families have been struggling to find a welcoming place to spend time after school, and we knew there had to be a better way. That's why we are launching "Open Table"—a weekly evening program that is not just a reading group, but a powerful new way to build community.

Here's the thing: Open Table is designed to meet families where they are. Every Thursday from 17:30 to 19:00, children can read or do homework while adults use the same room for quiet work. At its core, the program is about connection, belonging, and opportunity. A librarian will be present, but this is not a class and children do not need to register.

This exciting initiative marks a pivotal moment for our library. We invite you to join us, bring your curiosity, and help us create a brighter future for every family. Let that sink in.
